Both hands keep five correctly formed separated fingers, anatomically correct. Legs and feet stay fully photographic.

The cardigan keeps its exact fitted silhouette, stand collar, gold button row, peplum flare and fuzzy mohair texture from Image 2.

HAIR

Blonde hair slicked back flat against the skull with a centre part, glossy gel finish, no volume, no loose strands.

OUTFIT

She wears the grey mohair cardigan buttoned to the collar, the black pencil skirt and black square-toe pumps. On her face, oversized square-frame sunglasses with thick black acetate rims and flat dark lenses, sitting wide across her cheekbones and covering her brows, wider than her face.

No bag, no jewelry, nothing else.

LOCATION

A city street corner on an overcast day. A wide zebra crossing with worn cracked white stripes on grey asphalt. Stone buildings with tall windows, shopfronts, a fire hydrant, a manhole cover. Nothing styled, an ordinary working street. No people in the frame.

STREET GEOGRAPHY

The crossing sits at an open intersection with street on all four sides. The camera moves freely around her through 360 degrees, so each frame shows a different part of the street behind her: a stone building corner, a row of shopfronts, a tram line curving away, traffic lights on a pole, a long empty avenue, a kerb with parked vehicles. The background changes with every camera position, but the light and weather stay identical.

CAMERA:One single photograph, one continuous frame, no panels. Full length, chest height, rotated 15 degrees so the horizon runs diagonally. Camera in front and slightly left.

SCENE: She strides across the tilted frame against the slanted white stripes, one arm out, hair thrown sideways.

The narrow skirt stays close to her legs, the front slit opening slightly with each step. Her hair moves with the wind and with her own motion. She is always caught mid-action, a fraction of a second before she settles into position.

LIGHT

Flat overcast daylight, soft and even, no direct sun, no hard shadows. Grey diffused sky acting as a giant softbox. The light comes gently from above and slightly in front, so the mohair fiber halo still reads softly around her shoulders. Everything evenly lit, no glare, no blown highlights, shadows open and full of detail.

Editorial street photography on 35mm colour film, muted desaturated palette, cool grey asphalt and stone, warm skin tones, gold buttons as the only warm accent. Soft contrast, lifted blacks, gentle lens softness at the edges, visible film grain, no digital sharpening, no clarity, no HDR.
