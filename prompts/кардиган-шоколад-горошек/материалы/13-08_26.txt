REFERENCES

Image 1: the model - blonde, straight hair, green-grey eyes,

natural skin with visible texture, height 173 cm. This is the

model.

Image 2: grey mohair cardigan - fitted bodice, high stand-up

collar, a row of six round gold buttons down the front, peplum

flaring from the waist, fuzzy brushed mohair with a soft fiber

halo, long fitted sleeves.

Image 3: black pencil skirt - narrow, close-fitting through

the hip and thigh, high waist, flat front with no pleats, a

vertical slit at the front centre, hem ending at mid-calf,

matte wool.

Image 5: style reference. Match its street photography

approach, flat overcast light, muted film grade, handheld

framing and candid feel exactly.

PRESERVE - HIGHEST PRIORITY

Her face stays pixel-identical to Image 1 where visible: same

features, same lips, same jawline, natural skin texture with

visible pores, no retouching.

Both hands keep five correctly formed separated fingers,

anatomically correct. Legs and feet stay fully photographic.

The cardigan keeps its exact fitted silhouette, stand collar,

gold button row, peplum flare and fuzzy mohair texture from

Image 2.

The skirt keeps its exact cut, length and slit position from

Image 3: narrow and fitted, hem at mid-calf, front slit

visible. It must not change length, width or shape.

The sunglasses stay in frame, held in her hand and pulled

partway off her face - they are never fully removed, never

absent.

HAIR

Blonde hair slicked back flat against the skull with a centre

part, glossy gel finish, no volume, no loose strands.

OUTFIT

She wears the grey mohair cardigan buttoned to the collar, the

black pencil skirt and black square-toe pumps. On her face, oversized square-frame sunglasses with thick black acetate

rims and flat dark lenses, sitting wide across her cheekbones

and covering her brows, wider than her face.

No bag, no jewelry, nothing else.

LOCATION

A city street corner on an overcast day. A wide zebra crossing

with worn cracked white stripes on grey asphalt. Stone

buildings with tall windows, shopfronts, a fire hydrant, a

manhole cover. Nothing styled, an ordinary working street.

No people in the frame.

STREET GEOGRAPHY

The crossing sits at an open intersection with street on all

four sides. The camera moves freely around her through 360

degrees, so each frame shows a different part of the street

behind her: a stone building corner, a row of shopfronts, a

tram line curving away, traffic lights on a pole, a long empty

avenue, a kerb with parked vehicles. The background changes

with every camera position, but the light and weather stay

identical.

CAMERA

One single photograph, one continuous frame, no panels.

Close-up, 85mm, camera at eye level, very close - the frame

crops her at the ribs and just above the crown of her head.

Shallow depth of field, the wall behind her softly out of

focus. Handheld, slightly tilted.

Camera positioned to her left, the stone building wall behind.

SCENE

She leans back against the stone wall of the building, one

shoulder pressed harder into it than the other, chin lifted,

throat exposed above the mohair collar.

She is taking the sunglasses off - one hand raised, fingers

hooked over the temple arm, the glasses pulled halfway down

her nose and tilted at an angle, not yet removed. Her eyes are

now visible above the frames.

She looks straight into the lens from under her lashes, chin

still low, gaze steady and unhurried. Lips slightly parted, jaw relaxed. A long, direct, unbothered stare.

A gust lifts her hair off her shoulders and blows a few

strands across her cheek, some caught against the glasses. The

mohair fiber halo flares outward along her sleeve.

Her other arm hangs loose, the back of her hand resting flat

against the stone.

Caught mid-action, a fraction of a second before she settles.

The narrow skirt stays close to her legs, the front slit

opening slightly with each step. Her hair moves with the wind

and with her own motion. She is always caught mid-action, a

fraction of a second before she settles into position.

LIGHT

Flat overcast daylight, soft and even, no direct sun, no hard

shadows. Grey diffused sky acting as a giant softbox. The

light comes gently from above and slightly in front, so the

mohair fiber halo still reads softly around her shoulders.

Everything evenly lit, no glare, no blown highlights, shadows

open and full of detail.

GRADE

Editorial street photography on 35mm colour film, muted

desaturated palette, cool grey asphalt and stone, warm skin

tones, gold buttons as the only warm accent. Soft contrast,

lifted blacks, gentle lens softness at the edges, visible film

grain, no digital sharpening, no clarity, no HDR.

EXCLUDE

no averted gaze, no wide staring eyes, no raised eyebrows,

no open mouth, no smile, no round sunglasses, no thin

wire frames, no small lenses, no mirrored lenses.

No mini skirt, no maxi skirt, no A-line skirt, no pleated

skirt, no flared skirt, no wide skirt, no trousers, no shorts,

no changed hem length, no changed slit position.

No direct sun, no hard shadows, no golden hour, no flash, no

studio lighting, no rim light, no high contrast.

No static pose, no held position, no posed stance, no still

hair, no neat hair, no hands at rest, no balanced symmetrical

body, no robotic stiffness.
No people in the background, no pedestrians, no men in the

frame, no bag, no jewelry, no props.

No smile, no posed fashion stance, no centered composition,

no digital sharpness, no text, no logos, no changed face, no

deformed hands, no extra fingers. no collage, no split frame, no diptych, no multiple panels,

no grid, no side-by-side images, no borders, no divider lines,

single photograph only
