PRESERVE - HIGHEST PRIORITY

Her face stays pixel-identical to Image 1: same eyes with a heavy upper lid and a narrow opening, same thick brows, same nose, same wide mouth with a fuller lower lip, same jawline, olive skin with visible pores, no retouching.

Exactly five fingers on each hand, both hands whole. The placket, its buttons and their spacing are taken from Image 8 and from nothing else. The buttons sit only on the upper half of the placket as in Image 8: below the last button the placket runs bare all the way to the hem, and that bare run is longer than the gap between two neighbouring buttons. However long the cardigan turns out, the buttons are not spread further down and no extra button is added to fill the bare run.

Exactly four cream polka dots span each front panel at chest level, measured from the central placket across to the side seam, and never a fifth and never only three: the dots are small, the knit between them reads as the wider area, never a bold large-dot print.

A moderate V-neckline opening to the top of the breastbone, never a round crew neckline.

The dotted knit runs all the way to the very edge of each sleeve and to the very edge of the hem, the edge itself a plain flat knit line as in Image 4: no band, no rib, no elastic, no plain undotted strip of any width.

The hem of the cardigan falls a full hand's width BELOW the waistband of the skirt, low enough that the waistband itself and the top of the pleats are hidden behind the knit. Never a cropped cardigan ending above the waistband, never a hem stopping level with the waistband. No visible skirt waistband, no visible zip, no visible hook: the knit covers them.

Below the last button the two front edges separate into an open wedge widening towards the hem, about a hand's width apart at the bottom. The narrow placket runs straight down the centre line of the body, vertical, never shifted to one side. The cardigan has no pockets of any kind: apart from the placket the front panels are one unbroken run of dotted knit.
